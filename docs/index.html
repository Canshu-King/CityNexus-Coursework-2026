A city trip that doesn't stop at planning.

What if a travel assistant could understand your goal, research the city, show its work, and help you adapt when a journey changes? CityNexus explores that idea through full-stack engineering, AI Agent research and controlled multi-day simulations.

Not just a list of features.

These three selected results show how we confronted actual reasoning, route-data and classroom-execution problems. Their evidence grades are deliberately different.

R41 · LIVE ROUTING PROVIDER

22-minute walking route

A fresh local Journey requested one Valhalla walking route between two Nara places. A real provider returned a 22-minute source-bound mobility fact. This was not a physical trip.
